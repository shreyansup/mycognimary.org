Medication Interactions — Read Before Ordering

Several ingredients in Cognimary carry documented interaction considerations that every potential buyer should know:

  • Blood thinners (warfarin, anticoagulants): Ginkgo Biloba may affect bleeding. Do not combine without professional guidance.
  • Diabetes medication (metformin, insulin, sulfonylureas): Alpha Lipoic Acid may affect blood sugar. Monitor more closely and tell your prescriber what you started.
  • Thyroid medication (levothyroxine): Bacopa Monnieri may interact with thyroid hormone. Worth a pharmacist conversation before ordering.

If you take any prescription medication, manage a chronic condition, are pregnant or nursing, or are under 18, consult your healthcare provider before starting Cognimary or any other supplement.
