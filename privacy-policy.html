Last updated: September 24, 2026 · Applies to mycognimary.org

We do not process orders, ship product, or handle payment information — those are handled by the official seller's checkout platform, which operates under its own privacy policy.

We collect only the minimum information needed to operate this website:

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

3. How We Use Information

  • To understand which sections of the review are most read, so we can improve future content.
  • To detect and prevent spam, bots, and abusive traffic.
  • To comply with legal obligations where required.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.
